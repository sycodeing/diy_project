# X candidate outreach groups

New image-preview auto-queue jobs resolve an outreach group when their material is prepared. The resolver in `src/lib/x-outreach-groups.ts` prefers the reviewed `targeting_data.purchase_intent.scene` (`custom`, `gift`, or `decor`), then `targeting_data.outreach_group`, `targeting_data.discovery_segment`, and finally the legacy `segment`. Unknown values use `general`. A discovery query segment is not automatically treated as stronger evidence than the post-level purchase-intent scene.

Each group has one label and two preview-reply templates in the `X_OUTREACH_GROUPS` registry. The existing stable A/B assignment chooses the template. The generated marketing link stores `campaign=xg_<group>_<YYYYMMDD>` and the A/B `variant`; because campaign participates in the idempotency key, changing a lead's group creates a different short link. The queue shows the group from the saved link. The marketing page and CSV derive group identity from the saved campaign, so later screening changes cannot rewrite historical attribution. Old X links remain `历史 X / 未分组`; unrelated campaigns are separate.

To add a group, add a registry entry with a URL-safe key (lowercase letters, numbers and underscores, at most 40 characters), a label, and both copy variants. Have the discovery logic persist that key as `targeting_data.outreach_group` or a compatible `purchase_intent.scene`/`discovery_segment`. No queue, link, or reporting conditional needs to be added. Test both templates for length and factual fit before enabling the new screening segment.

This change does not regenerate approved/sent jobs, run discovery, enqueue candidates, or send replies. Existing text-only drafts remain on their old contract until manually regenerated as image-preview drafts before approval. Marketing metrics distinguish generated links and raw redirects from browser callbacks, interactions and paid orders; group comparison does not prove a human click or sale.
